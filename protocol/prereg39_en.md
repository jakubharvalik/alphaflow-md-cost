## Question of the preprint

Does an AI generator ensemble (AlphaFlow-MD, Jing et al. 2024) plus short MD (S18) give a conformational ensemble close to ATLAS (3 × 100 ns) more cheaply than plain MD from the crystal on the same GPU, when the cost of the generator is honestly included? Expected (negative) conclusion from line 38 on 6 proteins: without the generator cost a median of ~1.7×, with it ~1× to 0.16×. The preprint is to test whether this conclusion holds in general and to show how the choice of accounting changes the verdict.

## Data

- Proteins: the test part of the ATLAS split of AlphaFlow/MDGen (`splits/atlas_test.csv`, 82 chains, SHA256 c5029649db0b7ff6…). The generator did not see them in training.
- Reference: ATLAS, 3 × 100 ns, CHARMM36m + TIP3P, frames every 10 ps, first 100 ps discarded.
- AI samples: the published AlphaFlow ensembles (huggingface.co/bjing-mit/alphaflow, samples), 250 structures per protein. Phase A: AF-MD, AF-MD+templates, ESMFlow-MD (whatever can be downloaded). Phase B: AF-MD+templates (`alphaflow_md_templates_base_202402`, as in line 38).

## Phase A: analysis of AF without MD (all 82)

- `af38.py` unchanged (SHA 1834af14…), n = 250, 20 ATLAS draws, 30 bins (and 15 as a check), trim 4, TICA lag 5 ns, 10 dimensions.
- Every protein that can be downloaded and converted. Failures (download, conversion, mismatch of AF and ATLAS length) are reported with the reason; nothing is dropped silently.
- Output: distributions of JS for core PCA and TICA, fraction outside ATLAS, Rama/χ1, RMSF correlation for AF against ATLAS and against "whole replica, 250" and "replica up to 10.9 ns, 250"; dependence on protein length.
- **Good AF** = core TICA JS (AF-MD+templates, n = 250, 30 bins) < 0.45.

## Phase B: S18 on MetaCentrum GPUs

- **Selection:** all 25 test proteins with seqres length ≤ 120 (no further selection, instead of the "24 stratified" from the card, so that the selection does not depend on the results of phase A): 7lp1_A, 7jfl_C, 6okd_C, 6ro6_A, 6l34_A, 6pce_B, 6odd_B, 6jv8_A, 6ono_C, 6in7_A, 7a66_B, 6h86_A, 7bwf_B, 6d7y_A, 6ypi_A, 6tly_A, 7s86_A, 7n0j_E, 6pxz_B, 6p5h_A, 6tgk_C, 6vjg_A, 6yhu_B, 7k7p_B, 6uof_A. Exclusion only for technical reasons (building p2 or the box fails, non-standard residues, broken backbone, missing AF samples), with the reason. A box that fails (NaN) is reported; S18 is valid with ≥ 16 boxes.
- **Protocol:** `s18.py` unchanged (SHA ec3ed937…): 18 ED targets from 250 samples (selection only from the AF ensemble, ATLAS is not used), box CHARMM36m + TIP3P, 0.15 M NaCl, HMR, dt 4 fs, minimisation, 80 ps with restraints (k 1000), 520 ps free MD; system p2_<N> from the ATLAS PDB via `build_system.py` (SHA 1022f4cd…). CUDA platform, GPU with compute capability ≥ 7.0.
- **Seeds:** primary analysis seed 0 on all 25. Secondary seed 1 (same targets, different velocities and noise) on all 25 after the primary runs have finished, in order of length; the number on which it finished is reported. Results for 6 proteins from the Mac (line 38) only as a reproducibility check.
- **Evaluation:** `sub38.py` (SHA 95df1583…) window 50–520 ps, core PCA and TICA; leave-one-out against the union of the two other ATLAS replicas (for each replica separately, mean over 3); `rel38.py` Rama and χ1; comparison rows "MD from the crystal 10.9 ns" and "whole 100 ns replica".

## Cost accounting (all on the same GPU, chosen in favour of S18)

- **MD from the crystal:** equivalent time t* from `sub38` (the shortest t at which the mean of the ATLAS replicas from the crystal reaches the JS of the S18 run at equal n), cost = t* / v_p2 + one equilibration, where v_p2 is the MD speed of the box p2_<N> measured in the same job (50 ps). If the curve does not reach the value of S18 within 100 ns, t* ≥ 100 ns: 100 ns is used as a lower bound (censoring against S18, correction Z2) and the speed-up is reported as "≥".
- **S18:** measured GPU time of the 18 boxes (minimisation + equilibration + MD). Building the boxes on the CPU is not counted in the primary accounting (favours S18); as a secondary result it is reported serially with the building.
- **Generator:** the published AlphaFlow time on an A100 (full version 38 s per sample, distilled 3.8 s; the reference length to be checked in the publication), rescaled to length L by the factor (L / L_ref)², with an A4000 counted as fast as an A100 (both favour S18). Primarily the full version and 250 samples (this is how S18 actually selected samples); secondarily distilled and 250, distilled and 50, and zero (the samples already exist).
- **Speed-up** = cost of MD from the crystal / (cost of S18 + cost of the generator), separately for core and TICA.

## Criteria (yes/no decisions)

1. **(i) Cost [replaced by amendment Z3 below]:** with the primary accounting (full generator, 250 samples), S18 gives a speed-up ≥ 1.5× in both core and TICA on **less than 50 %** of the proteins. Met = the negative conclusion of line 38 holds in general. The same is reported for all secondary accountings; only the primary one decides.
2. **(ii) Benefit of coverage on good AF:** on proteins with good AF, S18 in leave-one-out is closer to the two independent replicas than the whole 100 ns replica (both core and TICA JS smaller) in at least **75 %** of cases, and at the same time has Rama ≤ 0.08 and χ1 ≤ 0.08 (rel38, window 50–520). If good AF has fewer than 4 proteins, (ii) is not evaluated and only described.
3. **(iii) Failure check:** on proteins with bad AF (TICA JS ≥ 0.45), the fraction where S18 loses to MD from the crystal of 10.9 ns is reported (I expect the majority), descriptive only.

## What will not enter the preprint without further consent of the author

**9 Nov 2026 21:50 CET, implementation correction (not a change of criteria or method)**, after the phase-B results with the published cost, before the generator measurement:

- **MD speed on the GPU of job B:** prereg39 prescribes converting t* to GPU seconds with the MD speed of system p2_<N> on the GPU of the S18 job. The job b39.pbs, however, measured bench_p2_<N> only in the first job of a given protein; the second seed and K18 could run on another GPU and took over a foreign speed.
